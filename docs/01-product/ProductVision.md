# Sportora V2 - Product Vision

## Mission

Sportora is an AI-powered sports tournament ecosystem that helps players discover tournaments, enables organizers to manage professional events, and provides admins with tools to verify organizers and maintain platform trust.

---

## Vision

To become India's largest digital platform for discovering, organizing, and managing sports tournaments.

---

## Core Users

### 1. Player

- Discover tournaments
- Join tournaments
- Receive notifications
- Track registrations
- View match schedules
- AI recommendations

---

### 2. Organizer

- Create tournaments
- Manage registrations
- Manage teams
- Upload venue images/videos
- Apply for verification
- Recruit referees, umpires, volunteers
- View analytics

---

### 3. Admin

- Verify organizers
- Verify venues
- Approve tournaments
- Handle reports
- Monitor fraud
- Manage platform

---

## AI Vision

Sportora includes an AI assistant capable of helping players, organizers, and admins complete tasks through guided conversations while requiring confirmation before sensitive actions.

---

## Long-Term Goal

Become the one-stop platform for sports tournaments across India.
